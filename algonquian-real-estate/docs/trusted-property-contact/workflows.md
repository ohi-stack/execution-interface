# Workflows

* **Visit completed:** save condition observations and checklist, generate the report through the PDF integration, notify the homeowner, and retain protected photos.
* **Urgent finding:** create an incident, alert the coordinator, follow emergency contact order, log every attempt, and escalate if the owner is unreachable.
* **Estimate submitted:** notify the homeowner and keep scheduling blocked until approval is recorded when required by scope or threshold.
* **Authorization expiry:** the daily event identifies authorizations expiring in 30 days, notifies the client, and records delivery status.
* **Overdue visit:** the daily event marks scheduled visits overdue, exposes them on the dashboard, and writes an audit event for escalation.

Request states are proposed, approved, scheduled, underway, completed, disputed, or canceled. Incidents retain mitigation actions and police, fire, utility, insurance, or municipal reference numbers.
