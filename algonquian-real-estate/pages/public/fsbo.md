## Selling For Sale By Owner in Connecticut

Many Connecticut property owners choose to sell without a real estate agent. Selling For Sale By Owner can provide flexibility and direct communication, but it also creates additional responsibilities.

Algonquian Real Estate LLC works directly with FSBO sellers who want a straightforward discussion about their property and available options.

## Common FSBO Challenges

- Limited buyer inquiries
- Property maintenance while marketing
- Pricing uncertainty
- Showing coordination
- Paperwork and transaction management
- Understanding alternative sale structures

## Our Approach

We start with a conversation about your goals.

Every seller situation is different.

Some owners want a traditional sale.
Some owners want a direct sale.
Some owners are exploring seller financing.
Some owners simply need local assistance evaluating options.
