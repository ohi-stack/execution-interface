## Inherited a Connecticut Property?

Managing inherited real estate can be challenging. Families often face decisions involving maintenance, occupancy, estate administration, taxes, repairs, and future ownership plans.

Algonquian Real Estate LLC works with heirs and families who want to understand available property options.

## Common Situations

- Property is vacant
- Multiple heirs involved
- Deferred maintenance
- Out-of-state ownership
- Estate administration process underway

## Our Goal

Provide information and discuss practical property solutions while encouraging families to seek appropriate legal and tax guidance when needed.
