## Sell Your Connecticut Property Directly

Algonquian Real Estate LLC works with Connecticut property owners who want a practical, direct conversation about selling a house, multifamily property, vacant property, inherited property, or property that has become difficult to manage.

We are local to Connecticut and focused on respectful, situation-based real estate solutions. Some owners want a direct purchase. Some want time. Some want help understanding seller financing, estate property issues, deferred maintenance, or what to do when the property no longer fits their life.

Our first step is simple: tell us about the property and what you are trying to accomplish.

## We May Be Able To Help If

- You are selling For Sale By Owner.
- The property is vacant or under-maintained.
- The home has become difficult to maintain.
- You inherited a property and need options.
- You are an older homeowner considering a transition.
- You own a rental property and no longer want to be a landlord.
- You are open to a direct sale or flexible purchase structure.

## What Happens Next

1. We review the property information you provide.
2. We may contact you to understand your goals, timing, property condition, and preferred outcome.
3. If appropriate, we discuss possible options, which may include a direct purchase, seller financing discussion, referral, or another practical path.
4. No pressure. No obligation. The goal is to understand whether there is a fit.
