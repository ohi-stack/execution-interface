## Understanding Seller Financing

Seller financing is a transaction structure where a property owner agrees to receive payments over time instead of receiving the entire purchase price at closing.

Not every property or seller is a fit for seller financing. However, some Connecticut property owners prefer exploring flexible structures that may help accomplish their goals.

## Potential Benefits

- Flexible transaction structures
- Potential monthly income stream
- Expanded buyer pool
- Customized terms between parties

## Important Note

Every seller financing transaction should be reviewed with qualified legal, tax, and financial professionals.

Algonquian Real Estate LLC does not provide legal or tax advice.
