## Own a Vacant Property in Connecticut?

Vacant properties can create ongoing challenges including maintenance, insurance concerns, security issues, taxes, and deferred repairs.

Algonquian Real Estate LLC works with owners who are evaluating options for vacant residential and investment properties.

## Common Vacant Property Situations

- Owner moved away
- Inherited property
- Former rental property
- Deferred maintenance
- Property no longer needed

## Discuss Your Situation

Every property and owner situation is different. We encourage owners to share their goals so that possible next steps can be evaluated.
