# Permissions

## Capabilities

- `manage_algq_marketplace`
- `view_algq_marketplace`
- `edit_algq_marketplace_deals`
- `manage_algq_marketplace_settings`
- `view_algq_buyer_activity`
- `submit_algq_buyer_interest`

## Roles

- Administrator: all marketplace capabilities.
- Marketplace Manager: operational capabilities for deals, settings, and buyer activity.
- Verified Buyer: buyer-facing marketplace view and interest submission only.
