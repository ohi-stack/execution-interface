# “Become a Contributor to ONEGODIAN™” — Cinematic AI-Hosted Video Blueprint

## 1) Master Creative Direction

**Run time:** 2:45 (target range 2:30–3:15)  
**Format:** 16:9, Ultra HD (3840×2160), 24 fps  
**Delivery:** MP4 (H.264 high profile), stereo mix, embedded captions + sidecar SRT  
**Tone:** Professional, globally inclusive, public-safe, documentary-grade

### Positioning Guardrails (must remain explicit)
- ONEGODIAN is presented as a **founder-defined identity and conceptual framework**.
- It is framed as a **digital ecosystem**, **technology + education platform**, and **community-centered infrastructure initiative**.
- It is **not** framed as a government, nation-state, replacement religion, anti-religious entity, or political movement.
- Language must remain non-extremist, non-conspiratorial, and non-coercive.

### Visual Language
- Apple-style cleanliness + Netflix documentary energy.
- Soft gold edge lighting against deep navy environment.
- Two AI hosts in a premium studio with immersive wall displays.
- Floating UI overlays: network maps, identity layers, dashboards, contributor pathways.
- Minimal clutter, confident pacing, generous negative space.

### Audio Language
- Ambient score: subtle synth pad + warm piano textures, low rhythmic pulse.
- SFX: restrained UI sweeps/clicks, soft whooshes for transitions.
- Voice: calm, articulate, trustworthy; no hype cadence.

## 4) Motion Graphics + Design Specs

### Typography
- Primary: Inter / SF Pro equivalent (clean geometric sans).
- Headline size: 88–110 px (4K timeline).
- Body / labels: 42–56 px.
- Subtitle safe size: ~50 px with 75% black translucent box.

### Color Palette
- Deep Navy: `#071427`
- Midnight Blue: `#0D1F3A`
- Soft Gold: `#D7B46A`
- Cool White: `#EEF3FF`
- Accent Cyan (sparingly): `#5CD6FF`

### Camera & Transition Language
- Slow dolly-in for key statements.
- 6–9 second average shot duration.
- Transitions: optical fades, light sweeps, and masked parallax.
- Avoid glitch-heavy or aggressive cyberpunk motifs.

---

## 5) Subtitles / Accessibility

- Provide full subtitles in English (SRT + burned-in optional).
- Pace captions at 140–170 wpm readability.
- Ensure WCAG-friendly contrast for text overlays.
- Avoid flashing/flicker patterns.
