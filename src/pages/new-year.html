Calendar Standard

Genesis 01, 0000 OT corresponds to March 18, 2025 Gregorian. OT year rollover occurs every March 18. Gregorian dates remain the controlling legal reference for civil, financial, and institutional purposes.

OneGodian New Year™ is presented as a cultural, educational, calendrical, and internal recordkeeping observance within the OneGodian ecosystem.
