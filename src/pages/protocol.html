The OneGodian Protocol™

The OneGodian Protocol™ is an optional identity, semantic, and alignment framework centered on unity, clarity, respectful interaction, and interoperability. It is designed for human identity expression, AI and agent interaction systems, digital communication environments, and future interface layers.

  • Human Layer: voluntary identity expression and public interpretation
  • Semantic Layer: classification, metadata, search, and knowledge systems
  • Agent Layer: AI alignment, prompt behavior, and interaction standards
  • Interface Layer: robotics, dashboards, assistants, and user-facing systems

Compliance: The Protocol is descriptive and optional. It does not establish doctrine, require adherence, or supersede legal, institutional, or platform rules.
